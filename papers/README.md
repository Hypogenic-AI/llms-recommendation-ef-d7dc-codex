# Downloaded Papers

1. `2305.19860_survey_llm_recommendation.pdf`
   - Title: A Survey on Large Language Models for Recommendation
   - Authors: Likang Wu et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - Source: arXiv 2305.19860
   - Why relevant: Core taxonomy of discriminative vs. generative LLM-for-recommendation methods.

2. `2307.02046_era_llm_recommender_systems.pdf`
   - Title: Recommender Systems in the Era of Large Language Models (LLMs)
   - Authors: Zihuai Zhao et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - Source: arXiv 2307.02046
   - Why relevant: Broad survey of prompting, pre-training, and fine-tuning strategies.

3. `2203.13366_p5_recommendation_as_language_processing.pdf`
   - Title: Recommendation as Language Processing (RLP): A Unified Pretrain, Personalized Prompt & Predict Paradigm (P5)
   - Authors: Shijie Geng et al.
   - Year: 2022
   - Source: arXiv 2203.13366
   - Why relevant: Foundational text-to-text recommendation framing that precedes current LLM recommender work.

4. `2305.07001_recommendation_as_instruction_following.pdf`

   - Authors: Junjie Zhang et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - Source: arXiv 2305.07001
   - Why relevant: Strong early open-source instruction-tuned recommendation model.

5. `2307.15780_llm_rec_personalized_recommendation.pdf`
   - Title: LLM-Rec: Personalized Recommendation via Prompting Large Language Models
   - Authors: Hanjia Lyu et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - Source: arXiv 2307.15780
   - Why relevant: Shows prompt-based text enrichment can materially improve recommendation quality.

6. `2312.02445_llara_large_language_recommendation_assistant.pdf`
   - Title: LLaRA: Large Language-Recommendation Assistant
   - Authors: Jiayi Liao et al.
   - Year: 2023
   - Source: arXiv 2312.02445

7. `2404.11343_a_llmrec_collaborative_filtering.pdf`

   - Authors: Sein Kim et al.
   - Year: 2024
   - Source: arXiv 2404.11343
